# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

This policy covers **danyeloz.info** (the official website of the artist Danyel OZ) and **Danyel OZ Studio**, the private tool I use to schedule and post my own music videos to my own social media accounts, including TikTok. "I", "me" and "my" mean Danyel OZ.

The short version: the website doesn't collect anything about you unless you write to me. Danyel OZ Studio only ever touches my own accounts, never yours.

## Who I am

I'm Danyel OZ, an independent singer-songwriter based in Manila, Philippines. You can reach me through the contact form on the Connect page of danyeloz.info.

## The website (danyeloz.info)

**What I collect.** Nothing, unless you choose to send it:

- **Contact form and letters.** If you write to me through the forms on the Connect page, I get what you type there (for example your name, your email address and your message). I use it only to read and answer you. I don't sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.
- **Waitlists.** If you join a waitlist (for example for merch), I use your email only to tell you about that one thing.

**What the site doesn't do.** The music widgets on the site don't use cookies, local storage, ads or tracking of their own. They read my public song list and show players and links.

**Services the site uses.** Parts of the site are run by other companies, and they have their own privacy policies:

- **Google Sites** hosts the site. **Google Fonts** supplies the fonts.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the music widgets.
- **Cognito Forms** runs the contact form, letters and waitlists.
- **Music and video players** from Bandcamp, YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced "no-cookie" mode), Apple Music and Spotify. A player may set its own cookies once you press play.
- **Links out** to stores, social media, Patreon and PayPal. Once you leave my site, the other site's policy applies.

## Danyel OZ Studio (the posting tool)

Danyel OZ Studio is a private tool that only I use. It isn't open to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it. It runs on Google Apps Script under my own Google account.

**What it does with TikTok.** When I connect my own TikTok account (through TikTok's official sign-in), the tool can:

- read my basic TikTok profile (display name, username and the account ID TikTok gives the app), so it shows which account it's posting to;
- read my posting options (who can see a video, and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed), so I can choose them for each video;
- post the videos I approve to my own TikTok account, with the caption and settings I chose, and check whether the post went through.

**What it keeps.** The TikTok sign-in tokens are kept in my Google Apps Script project's private settings. My TikTok username, my choices for each video and the results of each post (for example the post ID) are kept in a private Google Sheet that only I can open. The videos themselves come from my private Google Drive folder.

**What it doesn't do.** It doesn't read, collect or store anything about other TikTok users: no followers, comments, messages or viewing data. It doesn't post anything I haven't approved. It doesn't sell or share any TikTok data, and it doesn't use TikTok data for ads.

**Disconnecting.** I can disconnect TikTok in the tool at any time, which deletes the stored sign-in. You can also remove the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

The same applies to the other accounts the tool posts to or reads (my YouTube channel and my X account): it only acts on my own accounts and only on what I approve.

## How long I keep things

I keep messages from the contact form as long as I need them to answer you, then delete them when I clear out my inbox. Danyel OZ Studio keeps its records until I delete them. TikTok's sign-in expires on its own if it isn't used for 180 days.

## Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete anything you sent me through the website. Just write to me through the contact form on the Connect page. Under the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 you also have the right to object and to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

## Children

The website is a music site for a general audience. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent me personal information, write to me and I'll delete it.

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
