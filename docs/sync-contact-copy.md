**Sync & Licensing**

Music supervisors, editors and producers: every song here clears with one signature, and I answer my own
email.

**Rights**

One-stop clearance. Danyel OZ, LLC owns 100% of the master recordings and 100% of the publishing, so one agreement clears both sides. Songs are registered with a performing rights organization.

**How it works**

To license a song, send a request with the song, your project, the media, the territory and the term. You get a quote within 3 business days. We sign a simple license, and the files are delivered after signing: WAV plus instrumental, clean, stems or cutdowns where available.

**What to include in your request**

- Song title (or two, if you can't decide; I get it)
- Project: name and a short description
- Media: film, TV, ad, trailer, game, online video, and so on
- Territory: where it will be shown
- Term: how long you need the license
- Timeline: when you need the files

**The steps**

1. Request: email the details above.
2. Quote: you get a quote within 3 business days.
3. Agreement: we sign a simple license. No forty-page puzzle.
4. Delivery of files: after signing, you get WAV files plus the instrumental, clean version, stems or
   30 s and 60 s edits where available. Other versions on request.

**Contact**

Email: daniel.vizkaino@gmail.com
Subject line idea: "Sync request: [song title] for [project]"

**Links**

  (until that page exists: https://dcvizcayno.github.io/danyeloz-embeds/sync.html)
- Download the one-sheet: `[SITES URL: one-sheet page]`

## 3. Adding it in the Google Sites editor

You do this yourself. The edits stay a draft until you click Publish.

### A. Add the text

1. Open sites.google.com and open the danyeloz.info site.
2. In the Pages panel on the right, click "Contact & Collaborations" (under ABOUT).
3. Scroll to the end of the "Media & Business Inquiries" section.
4. In the right panel, click Insert, then click "Text box". A new text box appears at the bottom of the
   page.
5. Drag the text box up so it sits right after "Media & Business Inquiries" (grab the handle on its left
   edge).
6. Paste the section from part 1 into the text box (Ctrl+V). If the formatting looks odd, paste with
   Ctrl+Shift+V and re-apply bold to the headings.
7. Set "Sync & Licensing" to the same heading style as the other section titles (select it, then pick the
   style from the text box toolbar, for example "Heading").
8. For each link: select the words "Browse the licensing catalogue", press Ctrl+K, paste the page address,
   click Apply. Do the same for "Download the one-sheet".
9. Add the one-line edits from part 2 to the end of "Beats Licensing & Instrumentals" and "Media &
   Business Inquiries": click into each text box, put the cursor at the end, press Enter, type the line.

### B. Embed the catalogue (optional on this page, recommended on the catalogue page)

Option 1, Embed code (lets you set the height exactly):

1. In the right panel, click Insert, then click "Embed".
2. Click the "Embed code" tab.
3. Paste this code:

   ```html
   <iframe src="https://dcvizcayno.github.io/danyeloz-embeds/sync.html" width="100%" height="900" style="border:0" title="Danyel OZ licensing catalogue" loading="lazy"></iframe>
   ```

4. Click Next, then Insert.
5. If the box looks shorter than the list, drag its bottom edge down until it is about 900 px tall.

Option 2, By URL:

1. In the right panel, click Insert, then click "Embed".
2. Stay on the "By URL" tab and paste https://dcvizcayno.github.io/danyeloz-embeds/sync.html
3. Choose "Whole page" if asked, then click Insert.
4. Drag the bottom edge of the embed down to about 900 px tall.

The one-sheet works the same way with https://dcvizcayno.github.io/danyeloz-embeds/sync-one-sheet.html,
but a plain link ("Download the one-sheet") is usually enough; supervisors print it from there.

### C. Check, then publish (you only)

1. Click the Preview button (the screen icon at the top) and check the page on the computer and phone
   views.
2. Check that the email reads daniel.vizkaino@gmail.com and that both links open the right pages.
3. When you are happy, click Publish (top right). Nobody else publishes this page; until you click it, the
   live site stays as it is.
