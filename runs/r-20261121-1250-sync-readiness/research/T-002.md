# T-002 Research: sync catalogue and one-sheet standards

Run: r-20261121-1250-sync-readiness. Role: researcher. Date: 2026-11-21.

**Decision informed:** which fixed lists, per-track fields, one-sheet layout, wording and deliverable names
the build uses for C-1, C-3, C-4, C-5, C-6.

**Answer (short):** use one fixed 20-mood list built from Epidemic Sound and DISCO vocabulary, a 12-genre
list fitted to the artist (max 2 per track), put rights and clearance contact first everywhere (DISCO says
these are the most important metadata), list Instrumental, Clean, Stems, 60 s and 30 s as the public
availability flags (TV mix and a cappella optional, "on request"), and show the PRO name publicly but keep
IPI numbers off the public pages (they are public anyway, so no gain and no need).

### R-3 Per-track fields

**Must-have (show in catalogue and filter on):**
1. Title, artist, duration.
2. Mood (from R-1), genre (from R-2).
3. BPM (whole number) and key.
4. Vocal type: Male Vocal, Female Vocal, Duet, Instrumental (DISCO has a "Vocals" tag category).
5. Language: English, Spanish, French, Instrumental (lyric language is a hard filter for briefs; inference
   supported by translations note in sync-ready guides).
6. Explicit yes/no (a clean version is required "if there are explicit lyrics": BMI via mn2s).
7. Availability: Instrumental, Clean, Stems, 60 s, 30 s.
8. Rights status: one-stop, 100% master and 100% publishing, plus clearance contact (DISCO: most important).
9. Writers and producers (Guild panel: name the publisher and label; mn2s: composer and publisher details).
10. Listen link (streaming link, not attachments: Orphiq, Guild panel).

**Nice-to-have (show on detail or one-sheet, not filters):**
ISRC (identifies the recording; useful at clearance, mn2s/Orphiq checklist), ISWC, lyric themes
(DISCO "Lyrical theme" category; Orphiq lists lyrical themes), similar artists / reference artists (Orphiq),
instrumentation keywords, release year, notable placements.

Note: "one-stop" is a catalogue-level fact here (same for every song), so a single settings value is enough;
a per-track override is only needed if a cover or co-write appears later. DISCO says covers must name the
original and the publishing owner.

### R-4 One-sheet contents

One page (A4/Letter), in this order:
1. Artist name, one-line descriptor (genres, languages), small photo or logo optional.
2. Rights line: one-stop, 100% master and publishing, entity name, PRO registered.
3. Contact: licensing email, reply time (3 business days), catalogue link.
4. Short bio: 2 to 3 sentences (Orphiq). No streaming-number bragging (Orphiq), no "so syncable" (Guild panel).
5. Featured tracks table, 8 to 10 rows: title, duration, mood, genre, BPM, key, vocal/language, explicit,
   availability badges, listen link or QR.
6. Footer: deliverables available on request (WAV, instrumental, clean, stems, cutdowns), notable placements
   if any.

Common mistakes to design against: no rights information (Orphiq); attached MP3s instead of links (Orphiq,
Guild panel); long life story (Orphiq, Guild panel "shortest amount of time"); unfinished or unmastered songs
(Music Gateway, via search summary); uncleared samples (sync guides); lyrics over sections that should carry
voice-over, so offer instrumentals (Sonicbids, via search summary).

The 8 to 10 track count is not set by any source I read; sources describe one-sheets per track or per
artist. Keep 8 to 10 (charter C-4) as a design choice; it fits one page.

### R-5 Wording options (no em dashes)

**Rights / one-stop statement**
- A: "One-stop clearance. Danyel OZ, LLC owns 100% of the master recordings and 100% of the publishing, so
  one agreement clears both sides. Songs are registered with {PRO}."
- B: "Easy to clear: Danyel OZ, LLC controls 100% master and 100% publishing on every song here. One
  signature, one invoice. Registered with {PRO}."
  (Pattern follows DISCO's example: "One stop clearance, 100% master and 100% publishing control".
  When the PRO setting is blank, drop the last sentence or use "Registered with a performing rights
  organization.")

**Licensing process with turnaround**
- A: "How licensing works: 1. Email the song, the project, the media, territory and term. 2. You get a quote
  within 3 business days. 3. We sign a simple license agreement. 4. Files arrive the same day: WAV, plus
  instrumental, clean version, stems or cutdowns where available."
- B: "Send a request with the song title, where it will be used and for how long. I reply within 3 business
  days with a quote. Once the license is signed, I deliver high-quality WAV files and any alternate versions
  you need."
  ("Same day" delivery in A is a promise Daniel must confirm; drop it if unsure.)

### R-6 Deliverable names

Standard names (BMI, Music Connection, mn2s, Evolution Media):
- **Full Mix** (main version)
- **Instrumental** (rebalanced, not just vocal muted)
- **Clean** (also "Clean Edit", "Radio Edit", "Non-Explicit"; only needed if explicit)
- **Stems** (drums, bass, keys/guitars, synths, lead vocal, backing vocals, FX)
- **60 s edit** and **30 s edit** (cutdowns for ads; definition varies by client)
- **TV Mix** (instrumental keeping backing vocals, no lead vocal)
- **A Cappella** (vocals only)
- **Sting / Underscore** (library extras)

**List publicly as flags:** Instrumental, Clean, Stems, 60 s, 30 s (matches C-1). Mention TV Mix and
A Cappella only as "other versions on request" text; stings/underscore are library-only, skip.
Use "Clean" as the badge label and "Clean (radio edit)" in long text.

### R-7 PRO and IPI/CAE publicly

- **PRO name: yes, normal and useful.** DISCO's composer format includes PRO; the Guild panel wants music
  registered before pitching; mn2s lists PRO affiliation as metadata.
- **IPI/CAE numbers: not needed publicly.** They are public by design (ASCAP/BMI repertory, BMI Songview
  show them; MLC/TuneCore/BMI describe IPI as a public identifier), so listing them is harmless but no
  supervisor guide asks for them on a one-sheet or site. They go in the license paperwork and cue sheets.
  Recommend: show PRO name only; keep IPI as an optional private setting. Confidence: medium (no PRO
  statement found on posting IPI on personal sites).
